# Every stock, in points -- the full table

The companion to page 7 of `docs/validation/trader_pack.md`. **A point is one rupee of price on one share**: the per-share move each trade made, signed by side (`exit - entry` on a long, `entry - exit` on a short), summed per stock. It is computed post-hoc from the completed run's ledger and it is INDEPENDENT OF POSITION SIZE, which is what the trader asked for in Round 4 in place of his own capital question (QUESTIONS.md, ROUND-4 RECEIPTS, 06-Aug-2026).

**204 stocks ranked: even a no-edge system shows about 10 'good' ones by chance, at the conventional one-in-twenty rate. Treat the top entries as candidates for forward checking, never as proof.**

**No cost is subtracted anywhere on this page, and none can be**: a flat cost per trade is a rupee amount, and converting it to points needs a position size this view refuses to consult. At one share the round-trip cost of Rs 100.00 is 100.00 points; at three hundred shares it is a third of a point. The money after costs and size is `docs/reports/chunk9b_backtest_report.md` and page 1 of the pack.
