**2 of the 2 days that alerted are named LOUDLY** -- the headline carries THE EXCHANGE'S RECORD REFUSES and the instruction *"treat them as withdrawn"*, and `RefreshReport.render()` appends that line AFTER its READY verdict so an operator who stops reading at READY still sees it. There are 0 days that alerted and were refused without being named, which is the number that must stay zero.

The third day is the other half of the same rule and it matters as much: the oracle refuses it too, and because the tool never alerted on it the verdict is REPORTED and not shouted. A screen that shouted every refusal would spend, on the ordinary disclosed residual, exactly the attention the one alerted-then-refused morning needs.
